Overview

TagThemAll Bot ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our WhatsApp automation platform and related services.

By using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Our platform helps businesses automate WhatsApp communications, manage groups, and streamline customer interactions. We understand the sensitive nature of communication data and have implemented robust measures to protect your privacy.

Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our services:

Personal Information

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and billing information
  • Profile Data: Company name, business type, and communication preferences
  • Authentication Data: Login credentials and security verification information

WhatsApp Data

  • Contact Information: Phone numbers and names of WhatsApp contacts you interact with
  • Message Metadata: Timestamps, delivery status, and message types (text, media, etc.)
  • Group Information: Group names, participant lists, and group settings
  • Session Data: WhatsApp session tokens and connection status

Technical Information

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Features used, time spent on platform, and interaction patterns
  • Log Data: Server logs, error reports, and performance metrics
  • API Usage: API calls made, endpoints accessed, and response times

Payment Information

  • Billing Data: Subscription plans, payment history, and invoice information
  • Payment Methods: Credit card information (processed securely through Paystack)
  • Transaction Records: Payment confirmations and refund details

We never store your actual WhatsApp messages. We only process metadata necessary for automation features.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

Service Provision

  • Provide WhatsApp automation and group management features
  • Maintain and manage your WhatsApp sessions
  • Process and deliver automated messages
  • Synchronize contacts and group information

Platform Operations

  • Authenticate users and maintain account security
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Monitor system performance and prevent abuse
  • Provide customer support and technical assistance

Improvements and Analytics

  • Analyze usage patterns to improve our services
  • Develop new features and functionality
  • Optimize platform performance and reliability
  • Conduct research and development activities

Communications

  • Send service-related notifications and updates
  • Provide customer support responses
  • Share important security or policy changes
  • Send marketing communications (with your consent)

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

Service Providers

  • Payment Processing: Paystack for secure payment processing
  • Cloud Infrastructure: MongoDB Atlas and cloud hosting providers
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage analytics to improve our services
  • Customer Support: Third-party tools to provide better support

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to:

  • Valid legal processes (subpoenas, court orders)
  • Government requests or investigations
  • Protection of our rights, property, or safety
  • Prevention of fraud or illegal activities

Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of the business transaction. We will notify you of any such change in ownership.

All third-party service providers are contractually bound to protect your information and use it only for specified purposes.

Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your information:

Technical Safeguards

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256
  • Access Controls: Role-based access with multi-factor authentication
  • Network Security: Firewalls, intrusion detection, and DDoS protection
  • Regular Audits: Security assessments and vulnerability testing

Operational Security

  • Employee Training: Regular security awareness and privacy training
  • Background Checks: Screening of personnel with data access
  • Incident Response: 24/7 monitoring and rapid response procedures
  • Data Minimization: We collect only necessary information

Infrastructure Security

  • Secure Hosting: Enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure
  • Backup Systems: Regular encrypted backups with disaster recovery
  • Monitoring: Continuous system monitoring and alerting
  • Updates: Regular security patches and system updates

While we implement strong security measures, no system is 100% secure. Please use strong passwords and keep your account credentials confidential.

WhatsApp Integration

Our platform integrates with WhatsApp Web to provide automation features. Here's how we handle WhatsApp data:

Session Management

  • We maintain secure connections to WhatsApp Web on your behalf
  • Session tokens are encrypted and stored securely
  • You can disconnect sessions at any time through our dashboard
  • Sessions automatically expire based on WhatsApp's policies

Message Processing

  • No Message Storage: We do not store the content of your WhatsApp messages
  • Metadata Only: We process only necessary metadata for automation
  • Real-time Processing: Messages are processed in real-time and not retained
  • Contact Sync: Contact information is stored to enable automation features

WhatsApp Terms Compliance

Our service operates within WhatsApp's Terms of Service and Business Policy. We:

  • Do not send spam or unsolicited messages
  • Respect WhatsApp's rate limits and usage policies
  • Provide tools to help you comply with messaging regulations
  • Monitor for abuse and take corrective action when necessary

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience and improve our services:

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic platform functionality and security
  • Performance Cookies: Help us understand how you use our platform
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings
  • Analytics Cookies: Provide insights into usage patterns and performance

Third-Party Cookies

  • Google Analytics: Website traffic and user behavior analysis
  • Paystack: Payment processing and fraud prevention
  • Customer Support: Live chat and support ticket management

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through:

  • Browser settings to block or delete cookies
  • Our cookie preferences center (available on our website)
  • Opting out of analytics tracking
  • Disabling non-essential cookies while maintaining functionality

Disabling essential cookies may affect platform functionality. Performance and analytics cookies can be disabled without impacting core features.

Your Rights

You have several rights regarding your personal information. Depending on your location, these may include:

Access and Portability

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Data Portability: Export your data in a machine-readable format
  • Account Dashboard: View and manage your information through our platform

Correction and Deletion

  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Account Deletion: Permanently delete your account and associated data

Processing Controls

  • Right to Restrict: Limit how we process your information
  • Right to Object: Opt out of certain types of processing
  • Marketing Opt-out: Unsubscribe from promotional communications

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights:

  • Contact us at privacy@tagthemall.com
  • Use the privacy controls in your account dashboard
  • Submit a request through our support system
  • We will respond within 30 days of receiving your request

If you're not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy:

Account Data

  • Active Accounts: Retained while your account is active
  • Inactive Accounts: Deleted after 2 years of inactivity
  • Deleted Accounts: Permanently removed within 30 days

WhatsApp Data

  • Contact Information: Retained while you use our services
  • Session Data: Deleted when sessions are disconnected
  • Metadata: Retained for 90 days for service optimization

Legal and Compliance

  • Financial Records: Retained for 7 years as required by law
  • Legal Holds: Extended retention during legal proceedings
  • Security Logs: Retained for 1 year for security purposes

Children's Privacy

Our services are designed for business use and are not intended for children under the age of 16.

Age Restrictions

  • Users must be at least 16 years old to create an account
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16
  • Business accounts should ensure appropriate supervision of younger users
  • Parents can contact us to request deletion of a child's information

If We Learn of Children's Data

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 16:

  • We will immediately delete the information
  • We will suspend or terminate the associated account
  • We will notify the account holder of the action taken
  • We will implement additional verification measures

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 16, please contact us immediately at privacy@tagthemall.com.

International Transfers

As a global service, your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own.

Data Processing Locations

  • Primary Servers: Located in secure data centers in the United States and Europe
  • Backup Systems: Distributed across multiple geographic regions
  • Service Providers: May process data in various countries worldwide
  • Support Teams: May access data from different time zones for 24/7 support

Transfer Safeguards

When transferring data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards:

  • Adequacy Decisions: Transfers to countries with adequate data protection laws
  • Standard Contractual Clauses: EU-approved contracts for data protection
  • Certification Programs: Privacy Shield successors and similar frameworks
  • Encryption: All international transfers are encrypted in transit

Regional Compliance

  • GDPR (EU): Full compliance with European data protection regulations
  • CCPA (California): Compliance with California Consumer Privacy Act
  • PIPEDA (Canada): Adherence to Canadian privacy legislation
  • Local Laws: Compliance with applicable local privacy laws

We continuously monitor international data protection developments and update our practices to maintain the highest standards of privacy protection.

How We Notify You
